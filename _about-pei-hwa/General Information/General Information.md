<p><strong><u>Applied Learning Programme (ALP): </u></strong>
<br>The focus of the ALP named C3 (competent, confident communicator) was
on communication. The objective was to equip every student with the language
skills to communicate confidently and effectively. Since its implementation,
we have witnessed more students being more confident and competent in their
oral presentations to the school population. We then did a major review
in 2024 and decided to rename the ALP to Voices@PHPPS in 2025. This change
signals a shift in focus. We are not just equipping the students with the
essential language skills for communication, but we want them to apply
these skills to have a voice to advocate for a cause.</p>
<p><strong><u>Learning for Life Programme (LLP): </u></strong>
<br>Our school’s Learning for Life Programme in Sports (LLP Sports) aims to
nurture students to be passionate sportspersons and upright, contributing
leaders with strength of character. The A3 Instructional Approach ensures
that students acquire broad-based sports knowledge, values and skills.
They then apply their learning through authentic platforms such as group
work, camps, CCA, VIA, Outdoor Education and learning journeys. Students
experience affirmation of their application of knowledge, values and skills
through reflection, celebration of learning and positive comments from
teachers and parents.</p>
